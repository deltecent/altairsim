# PAiA SS-100 — Phoneme word list

Source: [PAiA-SS-100-Speech-Synthesizer.pdf](https://paia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PAiA-SS-100-Speech-Synthesizer.pdf), PDF pages 35–40, fetched 2026-11-20.

The booklet ends with the Votrax datasheet. Its last pages, headed "Phonetic Programs", list
English words and the SC-01 phonemes that say them. The list is in three columns on each page,
in alphabetical order. This file copies it. The phoneme symbols are the 64 symbols of Table 1 in
[PAiA SS-100 Speech Synthesizer](PAiA%20SS-100%20Speech%20Synthesizer.md). The scan has no text
layer, so every entry was read from the page image.

**How to read a row.** The phonemes are in the order that the program sends them to the board.
A row that says `(use "aid" program)` or `(see "R" program)` has no phonemes of its own. It
names another row. A name that ends in `-2` is a second way to say the word.

**How the copy was made.** The words and the phonemes are as printed. That includes the commas,
the spacing and the printing errors. A table has one row for each entry, in the order of the
page: the first column from top to bottom, then the second, then the third. The page numbers
are the datasheet's own. The PDF page is the page of the scan. The straight quote `"` stands
for the printed curly quote.

**Checked by script.** Each phoneme is one of the 64 symbols, and the words of each column
are in alphabetical order. Where the printing breaks one of these, the row is copied as
printed and the notes at the end say so.

## Notes on the transcription

**Letter O and digit 0.** The font prints the letter O and the digit 0 alike. Every `O1`, `O2` and
`OO1` is read as the letter O, because the SC-01 symbols are `O`, `O1`, `O2` and `OO1`. The
booklet's `O1` is code 35 (Table 1 prints it as `O3`). The booklet prints `PA0` with a letter O,
so it appears here as `PAO`. A count of `PAO` tokens is a count of tokens only. It is not a
reading of the digit.

**Out of order in the print.** These words are not in alphabetical order on the page. The rows keep
the printed order.
